About Me (see product demo on my YouTube)

Hi, I’m Vesal—a machine-learning researcher and entrepreneur specialising in deep-learning computer vision for autonomous and connected vehicles. As Founder/CEO of AutoAware AI Inc., I translate driver-safety computer-vision research into deployable, privacy-conscious edge-AI systems and am now extending the same low-cost platform to forward-facing ADAS functions. I previously taught the graduate Applied Machine Learning course to MS and PhD students at Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic), Iran. Prior to this, I was a post-doctoral researcher in the Electrical Engineering Department at Sharif University of Technology in Iran, where I co-founded the Sharif Center for Information Systems & Data Science with Prof. Babak Khalaj. Earlier, I earned a PhD in Civil Engineering (Intelligent Infrastructure Engineering) from Iowa State University in Ames, IA, USA, supervised by Prof. Anuj Sharma.

Research Interests

Computer Vision & Machine Learning, On-Device AI Optimization, Edge-AI for Mobile and Embedded Systems, Human-Machine Interaction & Human Factors, ADAS, Autonomous Vehicles, Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)
