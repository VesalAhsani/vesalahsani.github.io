About Me

Hi, I’m Vesal Ahsani—a machine-learning researcher and entrepreneur specialising in deep-learning computer vision for autonomous and connected vehicles. As Founder & CEO of IAIST (Intelligent Automotive Interior Sensing Technology), I transform in-cabin driver-monitoring research into deployable edge-AI devices and am now extending the same low-cost platform to forward-facing ADAS functions. I also teach the graduate Applied Machine Learning course to MS and PhD students at Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic). Prior to this, I was a post-doctoral researcher in the Electrical Engineering Department at Sharif University of Technology, where I co-founded the Sharif Center for Information Systems & Data Science with Prof. Babak Khalaj. Earlier, I earned a PhD in Civil Engineering (Intelligent Infrastructure Engineering) from Iowa State University, supervised by Prof. Anuj Sharma.
