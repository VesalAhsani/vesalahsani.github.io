About Me (see product demo on my  Youtube)

Hi, I’m Vesal—a machine-learning researcher and entrepreneur specialising in deep-learning computer vision for autonomous and connected vehicles. As Founder & CEO of AutoAware AI, I transform in-cabin driver-monitoring research into deployable edge-AI devices and am now extending the same low-cost platform to forward-facing ADAS functions. I also teach the graduate Applied Machine Learning course to MS and PhD students at Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic). Prior to this, I was a post-doctoral researcher in the Electrical Engineering Department at Sharif University of Technology, where I co-founded the Sharif Center for Information Systems & Data Science with Prof. Babak Khalaj. Earlier, I earned a PhD in Civil Engineering (Intelligent Infrastructure Engineering) from Iowa State University, supervised by Prof. Anuj Sharma.

Research Interests

Computer Vision & Machine Learning, On-Device AI Optimization, Edge AI for Mobile and Embedded Systems, Human-Machine Interaction & Human Factors, Autonomous & Intelligent Systems, Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)
